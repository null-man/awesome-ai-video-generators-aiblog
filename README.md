# Awesome AI Video Generators 2026

A curated list of AI video generators and resources for creators, marketers, and publishers.

## 🎥 Text-to-Video & Repurposing Tools
- **Pictory** – Turn long-form blog posts into short, branded videos with stock footage and captions.
- **Vidnoz AI** – Create avatar-led videos for training, sales, and presentations with realistic voices.
- **Alors On Danse Video Maker** – Independent AI: two photos → 13s studio dance video ([site](https://alorsondansevideo.com); Paid; not affiliated with Stromae/TikTok).
- **Bad Times AI** – Independent AI dance effect: one photo → 14–15s portrait dance video ([site](https://badtimesai.dev); Paid from $7.99).
- **I Found You in the Crowd AI** – Independent AI scene effect: two photos → balcony/spotlight meeting video ([site](https://ifoundyouinthecrowdai.com); Paid from $7.99).
- **KDA Firestarter AI Dance** – Independent unofficial AI: one photo → 13s 21:9 solo dance video ([site](https://kdafirestarterai.dev); Paid from $7.99; not affiliated with Riot Games or K/DA).
- **A2O LITE NEON AI Dance** – Independent AI: two photos → 15s neon duo dance with reference soundtrack ([site](https://a2oliteai.org); Paid from $7.99).
- **dime dime baby ai** – Independent AI: one photo → ~5s 9:16 AI dance (Quince Classic or Party Groove) ([site](https://dimedimebabyai.dev); Paid from $7.99).
- **[PromptImage](https://promptimage.cloud)** — Free AI image & video studio with a prompt library (signup credits, multi-model). Freemium.
- **[GrokVideo Studio](https://grokvideogenerator.dev)** — Independent web app for text/image-to-video via Grok Imagine API (Free Trial, 20 credits). Not an official xAI or X product.
